Questions

Good questions, short answers

Do I need any software?

No. If you can send an email, you can use Tidy Books. Forward invoices as they arrive, or send a batch of receipt photos once a week.

What do I need to send you?

Receipts and invoices for what you buy, copies of what you sell, and your bank statement each month. Most banks let you download it as a file in a few clicks, and we'll show you how.

I'm behind on my books. Can you help?

Yes. Send us what you have and we'll catch you up for €49 per month of records, then keep you up to date from there.

Who files my returns with Revenue?

We prepare every return and a qualified bookkeeper checks it. We'll agree with you at the start whether we file it on ROS for you or you file it yourself.

Why do you need my ID?

Irish anti-money laundering law requires bookkeepers to check the identity of every client. We'll ask for photo ID and proof of address when you join, and keep it secure.

Can I leave?

Any time, with a month's notice. There is no contract to sign up to, and we'll hand over your records in full.

Ready for tidy books?

Email us your name, your business and about how many receipts you have each month. We'll reply within one business day (Monday to Friday, except public holidays) with your price and how to get started.
